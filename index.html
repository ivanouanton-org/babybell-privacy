Privacy Policy

BabyBell: Baby Sleep Tracker

Last updated: October 9, 2026

BabyBell ("the app", "we", "us") helps parents and caregivers track a baby's sleep. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The app is provided by Anton Ivanou, Poland. Contact: antivdev@gmail.com.

Guest or account: your choice

As a guest (no sign-in), your baby's profile and sleep records are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our cloud. The app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics, for example that a sleep was logged. These events are not linked to an account or to your baby's name.

With an account (Sign in with Apple or Google), your profile and records sync to the cloud. You can then use them on other devices and share them with family members you invite.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Information we handle with an account

  • account details from Apple or Google: an identifier, email address and name (Apple may give us a private relay email);
  • your baby's profile: name, date of birth, estimated due date, sex;
  • sleep and wake records, including times, place and comments;
  • for family sharing: your role and relationship to the baby.

Information collected in both modes

  • usage events: which features are used, and general device and app details (model, OS and app version, language, approximate country);
  • crash reports;
  • subscription status. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive card details.

How we use information

  • to run the app: save records, show statistics, reports and sleep guides, send the reminders you turn on;
  • to sync data between your devices and with family members you invite;
  • to manage subscriptions;
  • to fix crashes and keep the service secure;
  • to improve the app. We may study de-identified, aggregated data, for example to check how accurate our sleep guides are. Results never identify you or your child. We look at a specific family's records only if you ask us to, for example in a support request.

Sleep guides and reports are general information, not medical advice.

Legal bases (GDPR)

We process data to provide the service you asked for, in our legitimate interest to keep the app secure and improve it, and with your consent for notifications. You can withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, cloud storage, analytics, crash reports;
  • Adapty: subscriptions;
  • Apple: sign-in, payments, notifications.

These providers process data on our behalf and may do so outside the European Economic Area, under the safeguards required by law. Family members you invite can see and edit the records you share with them. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete records in the app at any time.
  • As a guest, deleting the app deletes your records.
  • With an account, you can delete it in Profile. This deletes your account and cloud records. Some data may remain for a limited time in backups, analytics or crash reports, or where the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, and to object to some uses. Write to antivdev@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Children

The app is meant for parents and caregivers. Information about a baby is entered by an adult. The app is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new date.
