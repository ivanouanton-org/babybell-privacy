Privacy Policy

Baby Sleep Tracker – BabyBell

Baby Sleep Tracker – BabyBell respects your privacy. This app is designed for parents and caregivers to track baby sleep and wake time.

Summary: No account required. No ads. The app stores sleep data locally on your device and does not send it to our servers.

Information Collection and Use

BabyBell does not collect, store, or share personal information such as names, email addresses, contact lists, precise location, or health data.

The app may store sleep-related data locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Payments and Subscriptions

Subscriptions (if purchased) are processed by Apple via the App Store. The app does not collect or store payment information.

Third-Party Services

BabyBell does not use third-party advertising or tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

BabyBell is intended for parents and caregivers. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
